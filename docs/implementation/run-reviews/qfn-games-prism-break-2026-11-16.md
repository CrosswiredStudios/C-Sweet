## Why the publication handoff paused

The Producer's review instructions ask the model to decide whether the draft remains suitable. In the recorded requests after Victor completed the work, the serialized fields were `provisionalStory=null` and `provisional=null`. The review could see a completion summary, the epic, the accepted brief and changing resource facts. The two saved decisions selected the same Story and gave reasons to proceed. Neither identified a task or acceptance criterion that needed to change. The platform retained the full six-task proposal in Victor's completed session. [Review input](https://localhost:7033/api/core/organizations/729838c3-7be4-4cc7-bfd4-1c5c3dd847df/employees/bf5f4137-4d33-4cb0-b4dd-373905c0b8b2/timeline/83139f65-eaa5-ff20-3370-292616482dfe) · [Saved decision](https://localhost:7033/api/core/organizations/729838c3-7be4-4cc7-bfd4-1c5c3dd847df/employees/bf5f4137-4d33-4cb0-b4dd-373905c0b8b2/timeline/9606297d-b074-4349-a052-05edbe229b1f)

In the Producer, `AdvanceProgressivePlanningAsync` reads the completed proposal from `RefinementSessionId` for the pending Story. Its review callback receives the progress record. `ReviewAdaptivePlanAsync` builds the model's draft fields from `DraftSessionId` and `DraftStoryKey`. Those fields represent a previously retained draft and are populated after this decision parks the first refinement. Returning the same Story with `KeepDraft=false` and a different review basis takes the same parking branch as selecting a different Story. That explains the displayed “Current evidence changed the next Story” even though the Story key stayed the same. The review basis includes multiple inputs. The change in toolchain readiness is visible, but its contribution cannot be isolated as the sole cause.
