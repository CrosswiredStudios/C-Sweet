**Recommendation.** Give the Producer accountability for taking one player experience from an authorized idea to a staffed, playable increment. Put the current proposal, its accepted inputs, its owner and its outstanding conditions on the project board as soon as they exist. The platform can check whether a recovered proposal matches its assignment and whether the conditions to start are met. Reserve model judgment for choices that change the game or the plan.

## What made this fragile

1. **The recovery path lost its link to the work.** The Producer’s `RetryStory` cleared the pending Story and reset its refinement session to the last published session. No previous session had published a Story. After the manual retry succeeded, the persisted cycle still had one recorded retry, null refinement and draft session IDs, no detailed Story keys and no outstanding authority questions. It pointed to the direction-review session. The tool trace shows it read the same assessment key saved at 1:01 and deferred without a fresh planning model call. The successful reply was visible in Communications and absent from the facts used to resume publishing. The scoped management hold still needed an explicit disposition. [Producer’s planning task](https://localhost:7125/organizations/ea3b1a6d-0826-4ce9-9b28-876584a27785/employees/42993ff4-2604-4001-b1a9-208afc6d4293?tab=personal-board&item=36232586-94fb-4292-a76d-4134ccd8221e)

2. **Three components disagreed on what a decision meant.** Marcus chose `choice-0` and provided a rationale. The Director’s `PlanningDirectionRecorded` recognizes only `continue-current-plan` and `provide-direction` as completed directions. His task therefore described the Decided record as unresolved. The platform health reader independently treated a Decided record as a cleared dependency. It produced three incident records against the same follow-through task. Completing the decision, satisfying the conditions it imposed and restarting the affected work are three independently observable events. [Director’s follow-through](https://localhost:7125/organizations/ea3b1a6d-0826-4ce9-9b28-876584a27785/employees/f9a37596-ebb2-4ffd-a1c6-a502d75c9df5?tab=personal-board&item=7c3d2df8-55b9-42f3-b752-662da80fb00a) · [Platform dependency diagnosis](../../../src/CSweet.Infrastructure/Core/ProjectHealthReader.Dependencies.cs)

3. **The task-generation contract made recovery expensive.** I reconstructed the three model replies from the streamed audit. The first contained three research spikes, but its coverage array mapped only the description and omitted the criterion mappings. The second and third each placed a `video-game.feature.v1` Story where an executable child was required. Both named the right parent and were within the item-count bound. The last response ended normally after 11,317 characters. “Refine exactly the requested Story into 1 to 200 executable tasks” did not tell Victor to change the item type. Version 6.13.3 improved this diagnostic. That was a useful local fix, but the Producer still had to consume the recovered output.

4. **The plan approved technical leadership and quietly assumed implementation capacity.** Marcus’s staffing decision explicitly deferred an engineer. Gabriel selected the only eligible increment on the premise that Victor could handle it without a build. Both resulting briefs require an engineer, and the second depends on actual browser observations from the first. A Technical Director can advise on a renderer and review its tradeoffs. The staffing request for someone to implement the scene could have proceeded alongside that advice. This was a separate, still-unreached execution constraint.

5. **The roadmap amplified a reasonable uncertainty into a project-wide dependency.** This run’s exact planning inputs yield **316** nonempty source paragraphs using the paragraph rules in `GameOutcomeLedger.Read`. The pitch contributed 28, the GDD 265 and the brief 23. Victor’s inventory had **24 outcomes and 20 constraints**. Eleven Epics followed. The playtest protocol, technical decision record and playtest evidence Epics cite actual approved brief obligations. The accepted pitch itself specifies twelve levels, three balls and five music tracks. The CEO supplied the ambition and approved this elaboration. The avoidable design choice was to require whole-product traceability and to route all ten other Epics through the renderer spike before a single player interaction could be tried. For example, defining a short playtest question about ball visibility requires an agreed experience. Running that test requires a build. These deserve different dependencies. [Accepted production brief](https://localhost:7125/organizations/ea3b1a6d-0826-4ce9-9b28-876584a27785/documents?artifact=175a6661-1a49-46ed-b19b-1687342907b0)

6. **Monitoring consumed substantial effort while repeating the same question.** By 1:51 p.m. the ledger contained 6,308 completed-or-failed capability invocations, including 1,569 reads of agent-owned state, and 148 agent claims for periodic attention reviews. Of 71 completed provider attempts, 14 were manager-incident judgments. They consumed 484,717 of 1,571,362 recorded input and output tokens, about **31%**. These figures measure activity, not dollar cost. Five of the six failed capability calls were Gabriel trying to read delivery plans without the grant. Later he successfully read delivery plans. Those earlier denials warrant a capability check, but the trace at the stall identifies the old wait as the immediate cause.

## How I would have the team plan a game

| Decision or activity | Accountable participant | What the next participant actually receives |
|---|---|---|
| Agree what the player should experience. | CEO or commissioning owner sets the ambition and constraints. The game Director resolves product tradeoffs under delegated authority. | One approved brief with the promise, target, non-goals, creative quality bar and decisions reserved to the owner. Keep the longer design material available by reference. Let the team refine it as prototypes teach them something. |
| Choose the first demonstration and assemble the people to make it. | Producer, with a qualified implementer and the creative lead. Consult a Technical Director, audio specialist, accessibility specialist or other contributor where their judgment changes this slice. | A board Story with one observable outcome, a few acceptance observations, a named owner or an explicit vacancy, and only the dependencies that actually prevent it. Publish it as a draft immediately. A staffing vacancy starts a governed staffing request and remains visible on the draft. |
| Prepare work in parallel. | Implementer proposes the smallest build and estimates it. Creative lead gives one representative visual/audio reference and reviews legibility and feel. A qualified independent reviewer defines how to observe success. The Producer settles scope and staffing within authority. | Executable tasks, the dependency between them, the available people, and a repeatable demonstration. Put unrelated tooling, staffing and later content on their own tracks. Use estimates and capacity for committed work when they are available. |
| Commit and start. | Producer owns the proposal to start. The platform verifies scope, authorized assignees, capacity, dependency readiness, review coverage and the required environment using existing sprint preflight. | One Active sprint with a real assigned task and a worker claim. A failed check identifies the exact work, condition and person able to resolve it. Draft work can remain visible while this happens. |
| Inspect the result and choose what to do next. | The team plays the build. Creative direction judges feel, QA records observations and the Producer proposes the next slice. The Director handles material tradeoffs. | A build, the observations that changed the team’s judgment, and a decision to extend, revise or stop. Later content and release criteria stay on the product backlog until their evidence is needed. |

**For this game**, a first demonstration could be one short brick formation, a controllable paddle, one ball, one readable collision effect and one chiptune response. Have someone play it in desktop Chromium with effects enabled and reduced. Can they still track the ball? Does the combined hit feel worth repeating? The implementation can also produce the short renderer decision record the approved brief requires, with the Technical Director reviewing it. This is a proposed slice of the accepted game. The twelve-level arc and soundtrack remain product commitments. A materially different first increment would need the authorized manager to release or revise the existing pause.

3. **Split up the Producer’s work without hiring another manager.** Give draft publication, staffing, sprint preparation and exception reporting separately resumable commitments under Gabriel. 4. **Fix decision and validation contracts at both ends.** Give every manager option a typed effect, including a scoped hold with an owner and release evidence. Make Director follow-through and health monitoring evaluate that same effect. For planning output, let the model choose the deliverable, dependencies and useful acceptance evidence. Let code bind the parent, identities and source references. A recoverable failure should report what was rejected and the actual scheduled retry. Send one incident to someone who can act when recovery is exhausted or a real decision is required.
5. **Prove the transition through real worker pickup.** Replay this retained run from the public planning entry point with the old wait, cleared session pointer, manager hold and successful late result. Exercise offline agents, duplicate or out-of-order notices, two concurrent attempts, and a staffed or unstaffed implementer. Require exactly one board Story, the two existing briefs represented once, the hold reconciled by its authority, and one actionable staffing request if needed. Then exercise estimates, capacity, preflight, Active and first worker claim. On a fresh business measure times from pitch approval to executable ticket, staffed sprint, worker claim and first playable demonstration. Track human interventions, repeated incident reports and tokens per accepted increment alongside those milestones. A legitimate hold should pass the test by naming a reachable owner and leaving independent work available.
